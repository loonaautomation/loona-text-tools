How Loona Text Tools Work

  1. Choose a tool and add your text. Type it, paste it, or speak it - your words are read by your own browser only.
  2. Adjust the options. Case styles, sorting orders, limits and voices are all under your control.
  3. Copy or download the result. Results appear instantly on your device - copy them anywhere, or save them as a file.

Why People Trust Browser-Based Tools

Most online text sites ask you to send your writing - essays, letters, business documents, private notes - to a stranger's server. Loona Text Tools are built the opposite way: the processing code comes to your text, not the other way round. That means faster results, works-anywhere privacy, and nothing of yours stored, logged or shared. Close the tab and every trace of your words is gone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Loona Text Tools really free?

Yes - every tool is 100% free, with no sign-up, no watermarks and no daily limits imposed by us.

Is my text uploaded to your server?

Never. Every tool runs 100% in your browser. Your words stay on your device from start to finish - that is the whole design.

Which devices are supported?

Any modern browser on phone, tablet or computer - Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all work. Speech tools work best in Chrome and Edge.

Do the tools work offline?

Once a tool page has loaded, almost everything works even if your connection drops, because it all happens locally. (Speech recognition in some browsers needs a connection.)

Why a Roman Urdu to Urdu converter?

Loona is built in Pakistan, and millions of people type Urdu in English letters daily. Loona Automation's mission - Bolti Duniya - is that writing and reading should be open to everyone, in every language.
