Loona Crossword Solver

In short: type the letters you already have, put ? in every empty square - like c?oss?ord - and this solver searches all 172,820 words in the ENABLE dictionary for every answer that fits. Add must-contain letters to narrow the list further. Free, no sign-up, and 100% on-device.

Every crossword has that one answer with three letters missing and a clue that could mean anything. Instead of guessing, give the solver the shape of the answer: the letters you are sure of, in their squares, and a question mark everywhere else. It checks the full 172,820-word dictionary in a blink and lists every word that fits - usually a short list, and very often the answer is staring at you from the top of it.

Solve a pattern

Type known letters and ? for unknown squares - for example c?oss?ord. Or tap a length button to start from a blank pattern of that length, then replace the squares you know.

Every letter you type here must appear in the answer, in any position. Leave empty to search by pattern alone.

Private by design: everything in this tool happens 100% in your browser with JavaScript. The dictionary loads once into your device and your patterns are never uploaded to a server, never stored and never seen by us - close the tab and everything is gone.

How to use the Loona Crossword Solver

  1. Count the squares in your crossword answer - that is your pattern length. Tap the matching length button (3 to 12) to fill the pattern with that many ? marks, or type the pattern yourself.
  2. Replace each ? you already know with its letter, keeping the letters in their exact squares - a pattern like c?oss?ord has C, O, S, S, O, R and D fixed, with two squares open.
  3. If a crossing answer has given you a letter that must appear somewhere in this answer, add it under Must contain letters.
  4. Click Search - or just keep typing, results update live. Answers come back in alphabetical order with a count, so you can scan them against your clue.

Worked example

Your clue needs a nine-letter word and the crossing answers have given you C _ O S S _ O R D. Type c?oss?ord and search: the solver tests all 172,820 dictionary words of nine letters against those seven fixed letters, and the match list is headed by exactly the word you are reading about - crossword, with R in square 2 and W in square 6 filling the two question marks. The fewer squares left open, the shorter the list - two or three fixed letters usually cut thousands of candidates down to a handful.

Features

  • The full dictionary: every pattern is searched against all 172,820 ENABLE words - not a top-10,000 shortcut list - so uncommon but legitimate crossword answers are found too.
  • True wildcards: each ? stands for exactly one unknown letter, and fixed letters must match in their exact positions - the same rules as the grid itself.
  • Quick lengths: one tap fills a blank pattern of 3 to 12 squares, the lengths crosswords use most; longer patterns up to 28 letters can be typed directly.
  • Must-contain filter: require any letters to appear somewhere in the answer - perfect when crossings give you letters whose position you have not placed yet.
  • Live results: the list updates as you type, in alphabetical order with a clear count - and when no pattern is set, results are grouped by word length so you can browse by size.
  • Private and free: searches run 100% on your device after a one-time dictionary load. No sign-up, no uploads, no history kept.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do wildcards work in the crossword solver?

Type ? for every square you do not know yet - it stands for any single letter. The pattern's length is the answer's length, so c?oss?ord is nine squares long and matches CROSSWORD exactly: the letters you typed sit in squares 1, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8 and 9, and the two ? squares are filled by R in square 2 and W in square 6. Known letters must match exactly; only ? squares are free.

Which dictionary does the solver search?

The ENABLE word list - 172,820 English words in the public domain, long used by word-game and crossword software. Every pattern is searched against that full list on your own device.

What word lengths are supported?

Patterns from 2 to 28 letters - the full range of the dictionary. The quick buttons cover the most common crossword lengths, 3 to 12 letters; for anything longer, just type the full pattern with ? for the unknown squares.

What does the must-contain filter do?

It narrows the results to words that include every letter you list, somewhere in the word, in any position. It is handy when a crossing answer has already given you a letter that must appear in this answer too - for example, requiring an S and a T on top of the pattern s?????t.

Is my pattern uploaded or stored?

No. The dictionary loads into your browser and every search runs 100% on your device. Your patterns are never uploaded to a server, never stored and never seen by us - close the tab and everything is gone.
