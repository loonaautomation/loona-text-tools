    How it works

    An EPUB is a zipped set of XHTML chapters plus a package file (the OPF) that lists the correct reading order. This tool reuses the same tested parser as our EPUB Reader: it reads the container and OPF, follows the spine in order, cleans each chapter (scripts and unsafe markup are stripped), converts the cleaned HTML to text - block endings become line breaks, HTML entities such as &amp; and accented characters are decoded - and joins the chapters under their headings.

    Worked example

    You download a free public-domain novel from Project Gutenberg. The converter lists its 24 chapters, reports 88,410 words, and the downloaded .txt opens with the book title, the author, then Chapter 1 under its own heading - ready to paste into a word counter or a text-to-speech tool.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is this EPUB to text converter free?

    Yes - completely free, with no sign-up, no account and no limit.

    Is my ebook uploaded to a server?

    No. The file is converted 100% in your browser with JavaScript. It never leaves your device and is never stored by us.

    Why won't my book convert? (DRM)

    Most ebooks bought from stores such as Kindle, Kobo or Apple Books are protected by DRM (copy protection). This tool cannot and will not convert DRM-protected books, and it does not remove or crack DRM. DRM-free EPUB files open and convert normally.

    What is the file size limit?

    Books up to 20 MB are handled in the browser. A larger file gets a clear message instead of a broken conversion - try a smaller, DRM-free EPUB.

    Are chapter headings and order preserved?

    Yes. Chapters follow the EPUB spine (the publisher's reading order), each chapter starts with its heading, and the chapter list is shown before you download.
