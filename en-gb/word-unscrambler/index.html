How to use the Loona Word Unscrambler

  1. Type your letters in the box - any mix of A-Z, up to 15 letters. Results appear live as you type, or click Unscramble.
  2. Use ? for each blank tile: a ? stands for any letter, exactly like a blank tile in Scrabble or Words With Friends. For example, ca?e finds cage, cake, came, cane, care, case, cave and more.
  3. Read the results grouped by word length, longest first. Every word shows its Scrabble score - click any word to copy it.
  4. Too many results? Narrow them with the filters: Starts with, Ends with, Contains, and Minimum length - perfect for fitting a word around letters already on the board.

How it works

When you first type a letter, the tool downloads the ENABLE dictionary once and builds a fast index on your device, grouped by each word's sorted-letter signature. Your letters are signed the same way, and the engine checks which dictionary words can be built from your letters alone - using each letter at most as many times as you typed it, with ? tiles standing in for any letter. Every match is scored with the standard Scrabble letter values above, then grouped by length, longest first.

Because the whole search runs locally in your browser, it is instant after the first load, works the same on phone and desktop, and keeps your letters completely private.

Worked example

You type "tcaers". Add the filter Ends with "s" and a minimum length of 5, and only the board-ready options remain.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which dictionary does this word unscrambler use?

The full ENABLE dictionary - a public-domain lexicon of 172,820 English words that has been the standard open word list for word games and puzzles for decades. One honest note: ENABLE is a fixed public-domain list, so a handful of words added to the very newest tournament word lists may not appear here yet. Everything else - including the short, strange two- and three-letter words that win games - is in.

Is the Loona Word Unscrambler really free?

Yes - completely free, with unlimited searches, no sign-up and no daily limit. The search runs on your own device, so it costs us nothing per search either.

How do blank tiles work?

Type ? in the letters box wherever you have a blank tile - it stands for any letter from A to Z, just like a blank tile in Scrabble or Words With Friends. You can use more than one ? if you hold more than one blank. The score shown on each word is its standard Scrabble letter score; remember that in a real game, the letter a blank tile stands for scores 0 for that tile.

Is my input uploaded or stored?

No. The dictionary is downloaded once to your browser and every search runs entirely on your device with JavaScript. Your letters are never uploaded to a server, never stored and never seen by us - close the tab and everything is gone.

Why does this find more words than other anagram tools?

Most anagram and unscramble tools search a small built-in list of around 2,000 common words, so they miss the majority of what your letters can make. This tool searches all 172,820 words in the ENABLE dictionary, which is why it also finds the short, unusual words that basic tools never show.

What are the best strategies for winning word games?

Start at the longest group - longer words score more and clear your rack faster. Use the starts-with, ends-with and contains filters to fit words around letters already on the board. Watch the scores: Q, Z, J, X and K carry the most points, so build around them when you can. Save blank tiles (?) for a letter that completes a long or high-scoring word, and learn the two-letter words - they are the easiest way to play in tight corners.
