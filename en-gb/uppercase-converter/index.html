About this tool

UPPERCASE turns every letter into its capital form and leaves numbers, punctuation and spacing exactly where they are — the oldest text transformation in computing, and still the fastest way to format headings, labels and shouty announcements.

Be careful with readability: studies repeatedly show ALL-CAPS text reads 10–20% slower than mixed case, because the uniform word shapes remove the visual landmarks the eye uses. Use it for short labels, warnings and headings — never for paragraphs, where it is perceived as shouting.

Worked example

['A warehouse prints shelf labels from its inventory list: "spare parts bin 14" becomes "SPARE PARTS BIN 14" — readable from three metres, unambiguous, and typed once instead of re-keyed in capitals.']

Frequently asked questions

Will it change numbers or punctuation?

No — only letters are affected. Digits, spaces and punctuation pass through unchanged.

Is ALL CAPS hard to read?

For long text, yes — mixed case reads faster. Keep uppercase for headings, labels and short emphasis.
