Roman Urdu to Urdu Script Converter

In short: the Roman Urdu Converter turns Urdu written in English letters ('mera naam Ahmed hai') into proper Urdu script (میرا نام احمد ہے) - instantly and free.

Millions of Pakistanis type Urdu in English letters every day because Urdu keyboards feel slow - but Roman Urdu looks unprofessional in documents, assignments and official messages. Type or paste your Roman Urdu here and get clean, proper Urdu script you can copy anywhere.

Private by design: everything in this tool happens 100% in your browser with JavaScript. Your text is never uploaded to a server, never stored and never seen by us - close the tab and everything is gone.

How to use the Roman Urdu to Urdu

  1. Type or paste your Roman Urdu into the first box.
  2. Click Convert - common Urdu words become their exact script forms.
  3. Copy the Urdu text into Word, WhatsApp, your assignment or anywhere else.

How it works

Urdu spelling cannot be guessed letter-by-letter from Roman writing - 'hai' is ہے, not a sound-by-sound spelling. So this converter first matches every word against a built-in dictionary of the most common Urdu words, names and phrases - the words that make up everyday sentences - and converts those exactly. Words outside the dictionary are transliterated sound-by-sound using Urdu letter rules (kh → کھ, sh → ش, ch → چ and so on). Punctuation is converted too: a full stop becomes ۔ and a question mark becomes ؟. Everyday sentences convert almost perfectly; give unusual names or rare words a quick glance before sending.

Worked example

You type: 'Assalam-o-Alaikum, mera naam Ahmed hai. Main school mein ustad hoon aur mujhe taleem se mohabbat hai.' The result: 'السلام علیکم، میرا نام احمد ہے۔ میں اسکول میں استاد ہوں اور مجھے تعلیم سے محبت ہے۔' - ready for an official message.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Roman Urdu converter free?

Yes - completely free, no sign-up, use it as often as you like.

How accurate is it?

Everyday sentences built from common words convert almost exactly, because those words come from a real dictionary. Rare words and unusual names are sounded out letter-by-letter and may need a small correction.

Can it convert Urdu script back to Roman?

Not yet - this tool converts Roman Urdu into Urdu script, the direction people need for documents and formal messages.

Does it fix my spelling mistakes?

No - it converts what you typed. If a Roman word is misspelled ('mujhy' instead of 'mujhe'), the dictionary may miss it and sound it out instead.

Is my text uploaded?

No - conversion runs 100% in your browser on your device.
