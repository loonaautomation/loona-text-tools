Loona Scrabble Word Finder

In short: type the letters on your rack and this finder searches the full 172,820-word ENABLE dictionary for every word you can play, ranked by score - blanks supported with ?, an optional board pattern for words that must fit letters already on the board, and a one-word validity checker below. Everything runs 100% on your device: free, no sign-up, nothing uploaded.

A good rack hides more plays than most players ever spot - the seven-letter bingo, the short word that lands a Q without a U, the hook that fits a letter already on the board. This finder checks your letters against all 172,820 words in the ENABLE dictionary, scores every option with standard letter values, and puts the best plays first. Stuck on whether an opponent's word is even legal? The checker underneath settles it in one click.

How to use the Loona Scrabble Word Finder

Find your best plays

  1. Type your rack letters in the first box - for example aeiqrt? - using ? for each blank tile. You can enter up to 10 tiles.
  2. If your word must fit around letters already on the board, type the board pattern in the second box: the fixed letters in their exact positions, with ? or . for each open square - for example ?u??z. Leave it empty for a plain rack search.
  3. Click Find best plays. Results are ranked by score, highest first - ties go to the longer word, then A to Z - and each result shows its score in a badge. The count above the results tells you how many plays your rack can make.

Check if a word is valid

  1. Type the single word in the checker box - your own play, or one you want to challenge.
  2. Click Check word. A green verdict means the word is in the dictionary, with its face-value score; a red verdict means it is not in the dictionary.

Features

  • The full dictionary, not a sample: all 172,820 ENABLE words are searched on every lookup - the same complete list powers the finder and the checker.
  • Blank tiles done properly: type ? for a blank; it can stand for any letter, and that letter scores 0, exactly as on a real board.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which dictionary does this Scrabble word finder use?

It uses the ENABLE word list - 172,820 English words in the public domain, long used by word-game software. Every play and every validity check on this page is tested against that full list, not a shortened one.

Why is a word I know missing from the results?

ENABLE is a complete and generous list, but it is not updated every year. A few of the newest words added to the official tournament lists (such as the latest NWL or CSW updates) may be missing here. If a word matters in a serious game, confirm it in the current official list for your region.

How are blank tiles scored?

A blank tile scores 0 points, whichever letter it stands for. Type a blank as ? in your rack - for example, qu?z finds plays where the blank covers the missing letter, and that letter adds nothing to the score shown.

Is this an official Scrabble tool?

No. This is an independent helper from Loona Automation and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Hasbro or Mattel, the owners of Scrabble in different regions. It simply uses the standard English letter values (1 for A E I O U L N S T R, up to 10 for Q and Z) so the scores match the game you already know.

Is my rack uploaded or stored?

No. The dictionary loads into your browser and every search runs 100% on your device. Your letters are never uploaded to a server, never stored and never seen by us - close the tab and everything is gone.
