About this tool

Blog length is a trade-off, not a target: short posts (300–500 words) suit news and announcements; standard guides (1,000–1,500) answer one question properly; in-depth articles (2,000+) cover a topic completely and earn the links that drive search traffic.

The research consensus is consistent: length alone does not rank — coverage does. A 2,500-word post that answers the question fully beats a 500-word post that does not, and both beat a 3,000-word post padded with filler.

Worked example

A blogger drafts a "how to start composting" guide at 850 words. The counter places it in short-guide territory; she adds a troubleshooting section and a seasonal calendar, and it lands at 1,450 — the standard length for a topic readers search for.

Frequently asked questions

How long should a blog post be for SEO?

As long as the topic needs — search studies consistently find in-depth posts (1,500–2,500 words) earn more links, but only when the extra words add answers.

Do readers actually finish long posts?

Most do not read every word — they scan. Structure (headings, short paragraphs, lists) matters more than length.
