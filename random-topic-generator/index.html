Random Topic Generator - 900 Topics for Essays, Speeches & Stories

In short: pick a category and a level, click Generate, and get fresh topics for essays, speeches, debates, paragraphs, presentations or stories - drawn from 900 hand-written topics without repeats until the whole pool has been used.

Staring at a blank page is the hardest part of writing. This generator removes it: nine categories of ready topics, written by hand and sorted into kids, middle-school and high-school/college levels, so a teacher can find a morning-assembly speech topic and a student can find an essay title in the same click. Topics never repeat until every topic in the chosen pool has appeared once.

Your topics will appear here.

Private by design: everything in this tool happens 100% in your browser with JavaScript. The topic lists live inside this page, your favourites are stored only on your own device, and nothing is uploaded to a server - close the tab and everything is gone.

How to use the Random Topic Generator

  1. Choose a category - essays, speeches, debates, paragraphs, presentations, story ideas, kids, funny or school & college topics.
  2. Pick a level (kids & primary, middle school, or high school & college) and how many topics you want: one, or five at a time.
  3. Click "Generate Topics". Copy a single topic with its Copy button, copy the whole set with Copy All, or tap the star to keep a topic in your favourites.

How it works

Every category holds 100 topics written by hand for this tool - no scraped lists and no AI generation, so each topic is clean, classroom-safe and ready to write or speak on. Level tags keep the topics honest: a paragraph topic for a young child is a short descriptive subject like "My Pet Cat", while college-level debate topics tackle taxes, technology and public policy.

The generator uses a shuffle bag instead of pure chance. All topics in your chosen category and level are shuffled into a bag and drawn out one by one, so no topic can appear twice until the whole bag is empty - unlike simple random pickers that happily repeat the same topic three clicks in a row. Your favourites are saved in your browser's local storage on this device only.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this random topic generator free?

Yes - completely free, with no sign-up, no account and no limit on how many topics you generate.

How many topics are there?

900 topics in total: 100 in each of the 9 categories - essay, speech, debate, paragraph, presentation, story ideas, kids, funny and school & college.

Will the same topic appear again and again?

No. The generator draws from a shuffled bag, so a topic cannot repeat until every topic in the chosen category and level has been shown once.

Are the topics written by AI?

No. Every topic was written by hand for this tool and checked for level and classroom use - nothing is generated on the fly and nothing you do here is sent anywhere.

Can teachers use this in class?

Yes - that is what the level filter is for. Project the page, choose the class level, and let students draw their own essay, speech or debate topics fairly.
