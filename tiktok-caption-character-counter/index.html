About this tool

TikTok captions allow 2,200 characters in most regions (4,000 in a few), including hashtags — up to 100 per caption in some markets, though the algorithm rewards relevance over volume.

The caption is secondary on TikTok: the video and the on-screen text do the persuading, and the caption’s job is search keywords and one clear call to action. Keep it under 150 characters unless the video genuinely needs more.

Frequently asked questions

Do links and emojis count?

It depends on the platform — the notes on this page cover the common rules (for example, X counts every link as 23 characters). Emojis usually count as one or two characters depending on the platform.

What happens if I go over the limit?

Most platforms block posting, or truncate the text. The counter turns red and shows exactly how many characters to cut.

Is my text private?

Yes — counting happens entirely in your browser; nothing is uploaded or stored.

Do hashtags count against the limit?

Yes — hashtags are characters like any other, and count toward the 2,200 (or 4,000) total.
