Loona Wordle Helper

In short: type each guess you have played, tap its tiles to match the colours the game gave you - grey, yellow or green - and this helper instantly shows every English answer that still fits, plus the five best next guesses. Duplicate letters are handled exactly the way Wordle handles them.

Stuck on today's puzzle? This helper does not look up the answer and it never peeks at today's word. It simply does the hard bookkeeping for you: every green, yellow and grey tile across all your guesses becomes a rule, and only the words that obey every rule survive.

Tap a tile to cycle its colour: grey → yellow → green. You can add up to 6 rows, one for each guess.

Private by design: everything in this tool happens 100% in your browser with JavaScript. Your guesses are never uploaded to a server, never stored and never seen by us - close the tab and everything is gone.

An honest note about this helper

This helper never knows today's answer. It works only from the guesses and colours you enter, narrowing the standard English answer list by logic. If you want to solve the puzzle yourself, use it only when you are truly stuck - or after the game, to see which words were still possible.

The word lists are English. They are the lists the English daily game uses: about 2,315 common words that can be answers, and about 10,657 extra words accepted as guesses. Other languages play with different lists, so this helper is for the English puzzle.

How to use the Loona Wordle Helper

  1. Type a guess you have already played into a row - or add rows for up to all six guesses.
  2. Tap each tile until its colour matches the game: grey for not in the word, yellow for in the word but in the wrong place, green for the right letter in the right place.
  3. Read the results live: the best next guesses are shown first, followed by every answer that still fits, ranked by letter frequency.
  4. If the list suddenly becomes empty, a tile colour or letter is wrong somewhere - re-check the row that changed it.

How it works

Every tile becomes a rule, and all rows are combined before any word is tested. A green tile fixes its letter at that exact position. A yellow tile means the letter is in the word but not at that position. Grey tiles set the count for their letter.

Duplicate letters - the part most helpers get wrong: for each letter, count its green and yellow tiles across all your rows. If that letter also has a grey tile, the answer contains exactly that many copies - so one green E plus one grey E means exactly one E, never two. If the letter has no grey tile, the answer contains at least that many copies - so two yellow Es mean two or more. A letter with only grey tiles is not in the answer at all.

Surviving words are ranked by letter frequency: the helper counts how often each letter appears across the words that are still possible, then scores each word by its distinct letters. Words built from the most common remaining letters come first, which is why they make strong next guesses - each one tests the letters that matter most.

Worked example

Play SPEED and get grey, grey, grey, green, grey. The green tile fixes E in position 4, and the second E being grey caps the count at exactly one E - while S, P and D are out completely. Add ROBOT in all grey and R, O, B and T go too, leaving a much shorter list of words with exactly one E, in fourth place - words like HAVEN and CAMEL, which the helper shows you live.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Loona Wordle Helper free?

Yes - completely free, with unlimited use, no sign-up and no download. The whole helper runs in your browser, so it costs us nothing per solve either.

How do I use this Wordle helper?

Type a guess you have already played, then tap each tile until its colour matches the game - grey, yellow or green. Add another row for each guess. The list of possible answers and the best next guesses update live as you type and tap.

Does it handle duplicate letters correctly?

Yes - that is the hardest part of Wordle logic and this helper is built for it. If a letter is green or yellow in one tile and grey in another, the grey tile caps the count: the answer contains exactly as many copies of that letter as the green and yellow tiles show. A letter that is only grey is not in the answer at all.

Is this cheating at Wordle?

That is for you and your conscience - and whoever you share your score with. Many players use a helper only when they are truly stuck, or to check after the game why a word did or did not fit. The helper never looks up today's answer; it only narrows the list from the guesses you enter.

Which word list does it use?

It uses the standard English Wordle word lists: about 2,315 common words that can be daily answers, plus about 10,657 extra words the game accepts as guesses. Possible answers are drawn only from the answer list. The lists are in English, for the English daily puzzle.

Is anything I type uploaded or stored?

No. Your guesses are processed entirely in your browser with JavaScript. Nothing is uploaded, stored or seen by us - close the tab and everything is gone.
