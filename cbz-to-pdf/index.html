CBZ to PDF - Convert a Comic Archive into a PDF

In short: choose a .cbz file (or a .zip of page images) and get a clean PDF: one image per page at its full size, ordered naturally - page 1, 2, 10 in that order, not 1, 10, 2. Archives up to 20 MB, converted on your device; nothing is uploaded.

CBZ is simply a ZIP of scanned comic pages with a different name. This tool opens the archive, sorts the pages the way a reader expects, and builds a real PDF from them with pdf-lib - the same library our PDF tools use - entirely in your browser.

    How to use this tool

    1. Click the box above (or drag and drop) and choose a .cbz or .zip file up to 20 MB.
    2. Check the page list - it is the exact order the pages will appear in the PDF (natural numeric order).
    3. Click Download PDF and open it in any PDF reader.

    How it works

    The archive is unzipped locally with JSZip. Image files (.jpg, .png, .gif, .webp, .bmp, .avif) are collected, everything else is ignored, and the names are sorted with a natural-number comparison so numbered pages fall in reading order. JPG and PNG pages are embedded directly with pdf-lib; other image formats are first drawn to a canvas and embedded as PNG. Each PDF page is sized exactly to its image, so nothing is cropped or stretched.

    Worked example

    A 24-page scan named page-1.jpg to page-24.jpg converts to a 24-page PDF where page 10 follows page 9 - not page 1 - and every page keeps its original dimensions.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is this CBZ to PDF converter free?

    Yes - completely free, with no sign-up, no account and no limit.

    Is my comic uploaded to a server?

    No. The archive is opened and the PDF is built 100% in your browser. Your file never leaves your device and is never stored by us.

    What is the difference between CBZ and ZIP here?

    None for this tool - a CBZ file is a ZIP archive of images, so both extensions are accepted.

    Why is one of my pages missing?

    Only image files can become pages. A page in an unusual format that your browser cannot decode is listed as skipped instead of silently breaking the PDF.

    What is the size limit?

    Archives up to 20 MB are handled in the browser; larger files get a clear message.
